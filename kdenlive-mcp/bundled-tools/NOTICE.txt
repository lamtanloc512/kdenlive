Third-party binaries for kdenlive-mcp
=====================================
These are unmodified files taken from the official Kdenlive 26.08.3 release
(https://download.kde.org/stable/kdenlive/26.08/ - linux/kdenlive-26.08.3-x86_64.AppImage,
sha256 2c8322edfc86605e4c98a78a019310d9b4fc46bb8e94e6fe313dc7dd987e7671, and
macOS/kdenlive-26.08.3-arm64.dmg, sha256 22b385f1e2e5bf45c5bb6954bd58a97db34cead219a9a9ae6faa499f0096d71e),
as melt, ffmpeg and ffprobe plus the libraries they load:
  * macOS arm64: every MLT module the Kdenlive.app ships (so all of Kdenlive's effects and transitions render), the
    frei0r and LADSPA plugins, the Qt offscreen platform, Kdenlive's HD lumas and LUTs. Built by
    scripts/build-macos-tools.sh from the DMG's Kdenlive.app; frameworks keep their code signatures.
  * Linux x86_64: the MLT modules kdenlive-mcp needed before effects were supported (core, avformat, qt6, kdenlive,
    plus, xml, normalize, resample); not rebuilt yet.
Only the small bin/ wrapper scripts were added.

and the upstream projects above.
